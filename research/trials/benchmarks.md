Costs: 1 bp fee + 5 bp slippage per side. Signals at the close, fills at the next open. Long-only, cash only. Returns include dividends.

**Caveat:** Survivorship bias: universe is 19 of today's large-cap winners picked in 2026; free data has no delisted stocks, so stock-picking results are biased upwards.
